This is an informational website about Primarchs from the book series/tabletop game/video game series Warhammer. Most of the information comes from the book series Horus Heresy. To move through the website start by choosing the book you want and click or tap on it to see its content. When you are done with the book look at the bottom left corner where you will see an exit button. If a book has more content there will be a flip page button in the bottom right, click/tap on it to toggle through each of the pages. If you have any interest in the Warhammer book series the last book has a purchase form for the first book in the Horus Heresy ‘Horus Rising’.
